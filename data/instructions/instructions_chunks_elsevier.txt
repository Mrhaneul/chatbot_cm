PROBLEM:
Student cannot access Elsevier Evolve through Canvas Immediate Access.

APPLIES TO:
Platform: Elsevier Evolve
LMS: Canvas

CANVAS LOCATION:
Course Navigation > Immediate Access, then use the textbook tile to reveal the access code and open the provided URL.

STEP-BY-STEP RESOLUTION:
1. Sign onto Canvas and go to the course using IA.
2. Click the → Immediate Access link
3. Under the textbook tile, click → Reveal Access Code
4. Open the provided URL in a new tab.
5. Copy the provided access code.
6. In the new Elsevier tab, click → I’m a Student
7. At the bottom of the page, paste the code from Canvas into the Redeem an Access Code field
   - Continue to follow the prompts to access your materials.
8. You may need to create an Elsevier Evolve account using your CBU credentials.
   - Use your Name@calbaptist.edu email address
   - DO NOT use your ID#@calbaptist.edu address as you may not receive the necessary verification emails from Elsevier.

EXPECTED RESULT:
Student successfully accesses Elsevier Evolve content through Canvas.

IF ISSUE PERSISTS:
Contact ImmediateAccess@calbaptist.edu for assistance. If possible, include a screenshot of what you are seeing.
