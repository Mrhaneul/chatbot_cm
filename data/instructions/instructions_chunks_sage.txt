PROBLEM:
Student is opted into Immediate Access but cannot access their Sage Vantage courseware or chapter materials.

APPLIES TO:
Courses using Sage Vantage digital courseware (commonly used in Behavioral Science courses such as BEH 290 Introduction to Research Methods).

CANVAS LOCATION:
Course Navigation > Modules

STEP-BY-STEP RESOLUTION:
1. Log in to Canvas and select the course using Sage Vantage.
   - E.g., BEH 290-A
2. In the Canvas course, click into → Modules.
3. Under the weekly assignments, click the link to the textbook chapter through Sage Vantage.
4. After clicking into the link, register and create an account as a student using your CBU credentials.
   - Use your Name@calbaptist.edu email address.
   - DO NOT use your ID#@calbaptist.edu email, as you will not receive verification requests from Sage Vantage that may be needed.

LOGIN REQUIREMENTS:
- Use your official CBU email address for both Username and Email Address fields.

EXPECTED RESULT:
After registration, you should gain access to the Sage Vantage chapter materials, including reading content and video activities.

IF ISSUE PERSISTS:
Contact ImmediateAccess@calbaptist.edu for assistance. If possible, include a screenshot of what you are seeing.
