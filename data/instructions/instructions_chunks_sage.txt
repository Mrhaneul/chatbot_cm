PROBLEM:
Student is opted into Immediate Access but cannot access their Sage Vantage courseware or chapter materials.

APPLIES TO:
Courses using Sage Vantage digital courseware (commonly used in Behavioral Science courses such as BEH 290 Introduction to Research Methods).

BLACKBOARD LOCATION:
Weekly Chapters tab (left side menu)

STEP-BY-STEP RESOLUTION:
1. Log into Canvas.
2. Select the course that uses Sage Vantage.
3. Click "Weekly Chapters" from the left side menu.
4. Select the chapter you want to access (e.g., "Chapter 1").
5. Click on the chapter content link (e.g., "Chapter 1: The Purpose of Research").
6. You will be prompted to register and create a Sage Vantage account as a student.
7. Fill in your registration information:
   - First Name
   - Last Name
   - Country
   - Username (use your CBU email)
   - Email Address (use your CBU email)
   - Password (must be at least 8 characters with uppercase, lowercase, number, and/or symbol)
   - Confirm Password
8. Check the box to agree to the Terms of Use.
9. Click "Register" to complete your account setup.

LOGIN REQUIREMENTS:
- Use your official CBU email address for both Username and Email Address fields.

EXPECTED RESULT:
After registration, you should gain access to the Sage Vantage chapter materials, including reading content and video activities.

IF ISSUE PERSISTS:
Contact ImmediateAccess@calbaptist.edu for assistance. Please send your email from your LancerMail address and include your name, ID#, and course info.
