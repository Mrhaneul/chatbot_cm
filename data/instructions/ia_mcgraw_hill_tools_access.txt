PROBLEM:
Student is opted into Immediate Access but cannot access their McGraw Hill textbook or course materials.

APPLIES TO:
Courses using McGraw Hill Connect digital content (commonly used in Business courses such as BUS 212).

CANVAS LOCATION:
Course Navigation > McGraw Hill

STEP-BY-STEP RESOLUTION:
1. Sign onto Canvas and go to the course using McGraw Hill Connect courseware.
2. On the left-hand course menu, click → McGraw Hill to be pathed to your McGraw Hill Connect course.
3. On the right-hand side, you will be pathed to the McGraw Hill site, where you will be prompted to log into a McGraw Hill Connect account.
   a. If you do not have an existing account, create one with your Name@calbaptist.edu email address. DO NOT USE YOUR ID#@calbaptist.edu EMAIL.
   b. If you have an existing account, log in with those credentials.
4. Once logged in, you should be pathed to the McGraw Hill Connect course, where you can access both the eTextbook and the assignments below.

EXPECTED RESULT:
After following these steps, you should have access to your McGraw Hill e-book and can view your assignments.

IF ISSUE PERSISTS:
Contact ImmediateAccess@calbaptist.edu for assistance. If possible, include a screenshot of what you are seeing.
