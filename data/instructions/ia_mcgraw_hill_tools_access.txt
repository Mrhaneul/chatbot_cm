PROBLEM:
Student is opted into Immediate Access but cannot access their McGraw Hill textbook or course materials.

APPLIES TO:
Courses using McGraw Hill Connect digital content (commonly used in Business courses such as BUS 212).

BLACKBOARD LOCATION:
Tools tab (left side pane)

STEP-BY-STEP RESOLUTION:
1. Log onto Blackboard.
2. Select your course that uses McGraw Hill from your list of courses.
3. Click "Tools" from the left-hand side menu.
4. Select "McGraw Hill Connect LTIA Basic Link" from the Tools menu.
5. Once in McGraw Hill Connect, click the image of the book under "Course materials" in the Read section.
6. The e-book should deploy in the center of the page.

NAVIGATION INSIDE MCGRAW HILL:
- Course materials section: Access loose leaf options and your course text.
- Read section: Click on your textbook to open it.
- Assignments section: View weekly assignments organized by Week folders (Week 1, Week 2, etc.).
- You can also download the mobile app to read offline.

EXPECTED RESULT:
After following these steps, you should have access to your McGraw Hill e-book and can view your weekly assignments.

IF ISSUE PERSISTS:
Contact ImmediateAccess@calbaptist.edu for assistance. Please send your email from your LancerMail address and include your name, ID#, and course info.
