PROBLEM:
Student is opted into Immediate Access but cannot access the Bedford Bookshelf or included titles.

APPLIES TO:
Courses using Bedford Bookshelf titles through Immediate Access.

BLACKBOARD LOCATION:
Immediate Access tab

STEP-BY-STEP RESOLUTION:
1. Log in to Canvas and select your course.
2. Click the "Immediate Access" option from the left sidebar.
3. Click the Immediate Access link on the right-hand side.
4. The Bedford Bookshelf page will appear.
5. Click "Read Now".
6. Sign in to your Bookshelf account.
   a. If you do not have a Bookshelf account, create one using your CBU credentials.
   b. Use your NAME@calbaptist.edu email address.
   c. Do NOT use your ID@calbaptist.edu email address.
7. Enter the title of the material your course will be using in the search field at the top (Ex. Hello, Writer).
   a. Click → Open Book on the right-hand side to begin reading your material.

LOGIN REQUIREMENTS:
- Use your NAME@calbaptist.edu email address.
- Do NOT use your ID@calbaptist.edu email address.
- Using an ID email may prevent receipt of external emails such as VitalSource verification messages.

EXPECTED RESULT:
The Bedford Bookshelf library will load and allow access to the assigned course materials.

IF ISSUE PERSISTS:
Contact ImmediateAccess@calbaptist.edu for assistance. Please send your email from your LancerMail address and include your name, ID#, and course info.

PROBLEM:
Student is opted into Immediate Access for a Bedford Bookshelf title but cannot access the textbook after clicking “Read Now.”

COMMON SYMPTOMS:
- Clicking “Read Now” redirects to VitalSource Bookshelf but no book appears
- “My Library” is empty
- Student previously created a VitalSource account using an incorrect email address
- Account verification emails were sent to the wrong email
- System continues redirecting to the incorrect VitalSource account

COMMON CAUSE:
The student created multiple VitalSource (Bookshelf) accounts using different email addresses. Immediate Access materials must be linked to a single account created with the student’s full CBU email address (firstname.lastname@calbaptist.edu).

PLATFORM:
VitalSource Bookshelf (Bedford Bookshelf)

STEP-BY-STEP RESOLUTION:
1. Confirm the student is opted in to Immediate Access for the course.
2. Verify that the student is using their full CBU email address (firstname.lastname@calbaptist.edu).
3. If multiple VitalSource accounts exist, instruct the student to request an account merge.
4. Direct the student to contact VitalSource Support.
5. Ask the student to request a live agent through the support chat.
6. Have the student request that all VitalSource accounts be merged under their correct CBU email.
7. After the merge is complete, have the student return to Canvas.
8. Navigate to the course.
9. Click the “Immediate Access” link.
10. Click “Read Now.”

IMPORTANT NOTES:
- Bedford Bookshelf materials delivered through Immediate Access do NOT require publisher access codes.
- Immediate Access eTexts are codeless products in most cases.
- The Bedford Bookshelf is a library containing multiple titles. Students may need to search within the library to locate their assigned reading.

PROBLEM:
Student is opted into Immediate Access but cannot access their eTextbook through the Immediate Access tab in Canvas.

APPLIES TO:
General Immediate Access eTextbooks delivered through VitalSource or similar platforms (commonly used in English courses such as ENG 113 Composition with Bedford Bookshelf).

BLACKBOARD LOCATION:
Immediate Access tab (left side pane)

STEP-BY-STEP RESOLUTION:
1. Sign onto Canvas.
2. Go to the course page for your class.
3. Click "Immediate Access" on the left-hand side menu.
4. Click the "Immediate Access" link in the center of the page.
5. A pop-up should appear in a new tab showing your eTextbook information.
6. Click "Read Now" to access your eTextbook.

EXPECTED RESULT:
After clicking "Read Now," you should be taken directly to your eTextbook content in VitalSource Bookshelf or the publisher's reading platform.

IF ISSUE PERSISTS:
Contact ImmediateAccess@calbaptist.edu for assistance. Please send your email from your LancerMail address and include your name, ID#, and course info.

PROBLEM:
Student needs to create a VitalSource Bookshelf account to access Immediate Access materials.

APPLIES TO:
Courses using VitalSource Bookshelf.

STEP-BY-STEP RESOLUTION:
1. Go to Bookshelf.VitalSource.com.
2. Click "Create a VitalSource Account".
3. Select "I’m a Student".
4. Enter the required information.
5. Use your NAME@calbaptist.edu email address.
6. Agree to the Terms of Use and Privacy Policy.
7. Click "Create Account".
8. Select "Higher Ed".
9. Enter "California Baptist University" as the institution.
10. Click "Submit".
11. Verify the account using the confirmation email.

LOGIN REQUIREMENTS:
- Use your NAME@calbaptist.edu email address.

EXPECTED RESULT:
The VitalSource Bookshelf account is created and ready for use.

IF ISSUE PERSISTS:
Contact ImmediateAccess@calbaptist.edu for assistance. Please send your email from your LancerMail address and include your name, ID#, and course info.
