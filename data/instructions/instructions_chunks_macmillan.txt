PROBLEM:
Student is opted into Immediate Access but cannot access their MacMillan Achieve textbook, e-book, or assignments.

APPLIES TO:
Courses using MacMillan Achieve digital content (commonly used in Chemistry courses such as CHE 115).

CANVAS LOCATION:
Course Navigation > MacMillan Achieve

STEP-BY-STEP RESOLUTION:
1. Sign onto Canvas and go to the course using IA.
2. Click the → MacMillan Achieve tool on the left-hand side.
3. Select either → Achieve when you are ready to work on an assignment OR select → E-book when you want to review a chapter.
   a. If you have not used MacMillan Achieve before, you’ll be prompted to create an Achieve account first.

SUPPORT TOOLS AVAILABLE:
- MacMillan Learning Diagnostics: Run diagnostics on your MacMillan Learning connection.
- MacMillan Technical Support: Contact MacMillan technical support if having problems.
- MacMillan User Profile: View your MacMillan profile information or reset the mapping to your MacMillan account.

EXPECTED RESULT:
After following these steps, you should have full access to your MacMillan Achieve assignments and e-book content organized by week.

IF ISSUE PERSISTS:
Contact ImmediateAccess@calbaptist.edu for assistance. If possible, include a screenshot of what you are seeing.
