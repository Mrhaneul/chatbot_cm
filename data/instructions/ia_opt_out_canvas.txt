PROBLEM:
Student cannot access Immediate Access opt out through Canvas Immediate Access.

APPLIES TO:
Platform: Immediate Access opt out
LMS: Canvas

CANVAS LOCATION:
Canvas > Courses > course using Immediate Access > Immediate Access.

STEP-BY-STEP RESOLUTION:
1. Log onto Canvas and click → Courses
2. Click into the course using Immediate Access
3. Click → Immediate Access on the left-hand side.
4. Identify the IA title you’d like to opt out of, then click → Want to Opt Out?
5. Follow the prompts and confirm your Opt-Out.
6. After successfully opting out, you should receive an automated email to your Lancer Mail confirming Your Preference to Opt-Out Has Been Recorded.

EXPECTED RESULT:
Student successfully accesses Immediate Access opt out content through Canvas.

IF ISSUE PERSISTS:
Contact ImmediateAccess@calbaptist.edu for assistance. If possible, include a screenshot of what you are seeing.
