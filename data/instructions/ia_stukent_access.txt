PROBLEM:
Student cannot access their Stukent courseware, simulations (Simternship), or assignments in Canvas.

APPLIES TO:
Courses using Stukent digital courseware and simulations (commonly used in Communication courses such as COM 440 Digital Trends in Communication).

CANVAS LOCATION:
Course Navigation > Stukent

STEP-BY-STEP RESOLUTION:
1. Sign onto Canvas and go to the course using Stukent.
2. On the left-hand course menu, click → Stukent to be pathed to your Stukent course materials.
3. A new pop-up will appear. Click → Open Stukent.
4. Since this material is provided through Immediate Access, you should be able to launch your Stukent materials by clicking → Launch Simternship/Courseware

EXPECTED RESULT:
After following these steps, you should have access to your Stukent simulations, courseware, and quizzes. Since this material is provided through Immediate Access, no additional payment or access code should be required.

IF ISSUE PERSISTS:
Contact ImmediateAccess@calbaptist.edu for assistance. If possible, include a screenshot of what you are seeing.
