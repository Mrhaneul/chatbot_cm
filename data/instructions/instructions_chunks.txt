PROBLEM:
Student purchased a digital content (DC) code in-store at the Campus Store and needs to redeem it to access their eBook or courseware.

APPLIES TO:
Digital content codes purchased in-person at the CBU Campus Store (not through Immediate Access). This includes access codes for various publishers that were bought as physical cards or printed on receipts.

ACCESS LOCATION:
Campus Store Website - Bookstore.Calbaptist.edu

STEP-BY-STEP RESOLUTION:
1. Go to the Campus Store website at Bookstore.Calbaptist.edu
2. Click the Profile Icon in the top right-hand corner of the page.
3. Log into an existing account OR create a new account:
   - Use your CBU email: firstname.lastname@calbaptist.edu
   - Do NOT use your ID#@calbaptist.edu email address.
4. Once logged in, click "Activate and Access eBooks" from your account menu.
5. Click "Click here to activate an in-store purchase."
6. Enter the Redemption Code found at the bottom of your receipt.
7. Click "Activate" to redeem the code.
8. Once redeemed, your book will appear under "Other Digital Titles."
9. Click the dropdown "Access Content" next to your book.
10. Click "Access" to open the content page.
11. On the book cover page, look for a small pop-up in the top right corner. Click "Instructions."
12. A pop-up menu will appear. Click "Reveal Access Code."
13. Copy the access code displayed.
14. Click "Go to Website" under the access code OR visit the link provided by your instructor.
15. Paste your access code on the publisher's website to activate your materials.

PROBLEM:
Student is opted into Immediate Access for a Bedford Bookshelf title but cannot access the textbook after clicking “Read Now.”

COMMON SYMPTOMS:
- Clicking “Read Now” redirects to VitalSource Bookshelf but no book appears
- “My Library” is empty
- Student previously created a VitalSource account using an incorrect email address
- Account verification emails were sent to the wrong email
- System continues redirecting to the incorrect VitalSource account

COMMON CAUSE:
The student created multiple VitalSource (Bookshelf) accounts using different email addresses. Immediate Access materials must be linked to a single account created with the student’s full CBU email address (firstname.lastname@calbaptist.edu).

PLATFORM:
VitalSource Bookshelf (Bedford Bookshelf)

STEP-BY-STEP RESOLUTION:
1. Confirm the student is opted in to Immediate Access for the course.
2. Verify that the student is using their full CBU email address (firstname.lastname@calbaptist.edu).
3. If multiple VitalSource accounts exist, instruct the student to request an account merge.
4. Direct the student to contact VitalSource Support.
5. Ask the student to request a live agent through the support chat.
6. Have the student request that all VitalSource accounts be merged under their correct CBU email.
7. After the merge is complete, have the student return to Canvas.
8. Navigate to the course.
9. Click the “Immediate Access” link.
10. Click “Read Now.”

IMPORTANT NOTES:
- Bedford Bookshelf materials delivered through Immediate Access do NOT require publisher access codes.
- Immediate Access eTexts are codeless products in most cases.
- The Bedford Bookshelf is a library containing multiple titles. Students may need to search within the library to locate their assigned reading.
