PROBLEM:
Student needs to create a VitalSource Bookshelf account to access Immediate Access materials.

APPLIES TO:
Courses using VitalSource Bookshelf.

STEP-BY-STEP RESOLUTION:
1. Go to Bookshelf.VitalSource.com.
2. Click "Create a VitalSource Account".
3. Select "I’m a Student".
4. Enter the required information.
5. Use your NAME@calbaptist.edu email address.
6. Agree to the Terms of Use and Privacy Policy.
7. Click "Create Account".
8. Select "Higher Ed".
9. Enter "California Baptist University" as the institution.
10. Click "Submit".
11. Verify the account using the confirmation email.

LOGIN REQUIREMENTS:
- Use your NAME@calbaptist.edu email address.

EXPECTED RESULT:
The VitalSource Bookshelf account is created and ready for use.

IF ISSUE PERSISTS:
Contact ImmediateAccess@calbaptist.edu for assistance. Please send your email from your LancerMail address and include your name, ID#, and course info.
