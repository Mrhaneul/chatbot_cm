PROBLEM:
Student needs to create a VitalSource Bookshelf account to access Immediate Access materials.

APPLIES TO:
Courses using VitalSource Bookshelf.

STEP-BY-STEP RESOLUTION:
1. Go to Bookshelf.VitalSource.com
2. Click → Create a VitalSource Account
3. Select → I’m a Student.
   a. Fill out the necessary information to create the account.
   b. Be sure to use your Name@calbaptist.edu email address
   c. Be sure to agree to the Terms of Use and Privacy Policy.
4. Click → Create Account
5. Click → Higher Ed
   a. Enter the Institution name → California Baptist University
   b. Click → Submit
6. Verify the account through the email received.

LOGIN REQUIREMENTS:
- Use your NAME@calbaptist.edu email address.

EXPECTED RESULT:
The VitalSource Bookshelf account is created and ready for use.

IF ISSUE PERSISTS:
Contact ImmediateAccess@calbaptist.edu for assistance. If possible, include a screenshot of what you are seeing.
