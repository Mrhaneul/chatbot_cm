PROBLEM:
Immediate Access content does not load correctly due to stale browser cookies, cache, or history in Chrome.

APPLIES TO:
Students using Google Chrome.

STEP-BY-STEP RESOLUTION:
1. In the Chrome browser, in the top left-hand corner, click the three-dots icon.
2. Click → Settings toward the bottom.
3. In settings, click → Privacy and security.
4. Click → Delete browsing data.
5. In the pop-up, select → All time at the top.
6. Ensure all three options are selected: Browsing history, Cookies and other site data, and Cached images and files.
7. Click → Delete from this device.

EXPECTED RESULT:
Immediate Access content should reload with cleared Chrome cookies, cache, and history.

IF ISSUE PERSISTS:
Contact ImmediateAccess@calbaptist.edu for assistance. If possible, include a screenshot of what you are seeing.
