PROBLEM:
Immediate Access content does not load correctly due to blocked cookies or pop-ups in Chrome.

APPLIES TO:
Students using Google Chrome.

STEP-BY-STEP RESOLUTION:
1. Open the Chrome browser.
2. Click the three-dot icon in the upper right corner.
3. Click "Settings".
4. Click "Privacy and Security".
5. Click "Third-Party Cookies".
6. Ensure the following settings:
   a. Allow Third-Party Cookies is enabled.
   b. Block Third-Party Cookies is disabled.
   c. Block Third-Party Cookies in Incognito Mode is disabled.
   d. Send a "Do Not Track" request is disabled.
7. Return to Privacy and Security.
8. Scroll down and click "Site Settings".
9. Click "Pop-ups and redirects".
10. Under Default Behavior, ensure "Sites can send pop-ups and use redirects" is enabled.

EXPECTED RESULT:
Immediate Access links should open correctly without errors.

IF ISSUE PERSISTS:
Contact ImmediateAccess@calbaptist.edu for assistance. Please send your email from your LancerMail address and include your name, ID#, and course info.
