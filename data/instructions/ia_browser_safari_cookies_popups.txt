PROBLEM:
Immediate Access content does not load due to blocked pop-ups in Safari on macOS.

APPLIES TO:
Students using Safari on macOS.

STEP-BY-STEP RESOLUTION:
1. Open Safari.
2. Click "Safari" in the top-left menu.
3. Click "Settings".
4. Click "Websites".
5. Select "Pop-Up Windows" from the left panel.
6. Set all listed websites to Allow.
7. At the bottom, set "When visiting other websites" to Allow.

EXPECTED RESULT:
Pop-ups required for Immediate Access platforms will function correctly.

IF ISSUE PERSISTS:
Contact ImmediateAccess@calbaptist.edu for assistance. Please send your email from your LancerMail address and include your name, ID#, and course info.
