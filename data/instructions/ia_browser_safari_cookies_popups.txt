PROBLEM:
Immediate Access content does not load correctly due to stale browser cookies, cache, or history in Safari on macOS.

APPLIES TO:
Students using Safari on macOS.

STEP-BY-STEP RESOLUTION:
Clearing Cookies
1. In the Safari app, click → Safari on the top left-hand corner.
2. Then click → Settings from the menu.
3. Click → Privacy from the menu bar.
4. Click → Manage Website Data.
5. Click → Remove All from the bottom left-hand corner.

Clearing Cache
1. In the Safari app, click → Safari on the top left-hand corner.
2. Click → Settings from the menu.
3. Click → Advanced from the menu bar.
4. At the bottom of the page, click → Show Features for Web Developers.
5. At the top of the screen, click → Develop, followed by → Empty Caches from the menu.

Clearing History
1. In the Safari app, click → Safari on the top left-hand corner.
2. Click → Clear History from the menu.
3. Select → All History from the dropdown menu.
4. At the bottom right-hand corner, click → Clear History.

EXPECTED RESULT:
Immediate Access content should reload with cleared Safari cookies, cache, and history.

IF ISSUE PERSISTS:
Contact ImmediateAccess@calbaptist.edu for assistance. If possible, include a screenshot of what you are seeing.
