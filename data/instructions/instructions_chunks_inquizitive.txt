How To Access the Little Seagull & InQuizitive
1. On your Canvas course page, click -> Immediate Access on the left-hand side
panel.
a. Then click -> Immediate Access on the right-hand side.

2. Click -> Launch Courseware.
a. A new pop-up tab should appear. If no new tab appears, ensure that you
have enabled Third-Party cookies and disabled Pop-up blockers.

In the new tab, you will be pathed to the Norton platform.
3. Read/Accept the Terms of use and Privacy Policy and click -> Continue.

Following the How to Use InQuizitive tutorial, you should be pathed to the Little Seagull
Handbook home page, where you'll be able to access your eTextbook and InQuizitive
assignments.
