PROBLEM:
Student is opted into Immediate Access but cannot access their MacMillan Achieve textbook, e-book, or assignments.

APPLIES TO:
Courses using MacMillan Achieve digital content (commonly used in Chemistry courses such as CHE 115).

BLACKBOARD LOCATION:
Tools tab (left side pane)

STEP-BY-STEP RESOLUTION:
1. Log into Canvas.
2. Select the course that uses MacMillan Achieve.
3. Click "Tools" from the left side pane.
4. Select "MacMillan LTI Advantage Course Tools" from the Tools menu.
5. Choose your content type:
   - Click "Achieve" to access the Achieve homepage and work on assignments.
   - Click "E-book" to access the e-book that accompanies your MacMillan Learning course.
6. If you have not used MacMillan Achieve before, you will be prompted to create an Achieve account first.
7. Once logged in, you will see the Course Home with a Week Outline showing your assignments.

SUPPORT TOOLS AVAILABLE:
- MacMillan Learning Diagnostics: Run diagnostics on your MacMillan Learning connection.
- MacMillan Technical Support: Contact MacMillan technical support if having problems.
- MacMillan User Profile: View your MacMillan profile information or reset the mapping to your MacMillan account.

EXPECTED RESULT:
After following these steps, you should have full access to your MacMillan Achieve assignments and e-book content organized by week.

IF ISSUE PERSISTS:
Contact ImmediateAccess@calbaptist.edu for assistance. Please send your email from your LancerMail address and include your name, ID#, and course info.
