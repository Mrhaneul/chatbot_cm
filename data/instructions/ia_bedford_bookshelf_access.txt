PROBLEM:
Student is opted into Immediate Access but cannot access the Bedford Bookshelf or included titles.

APPLIES TO:
Courses using Bedford Bookshelf titles through Immediate Access.

BLACKBOARD LOCATION:
Immediate Access tab

STEP-BY-STEP RESOLUTION:
1. Log in to Canvas and select your course.
2. Click the "Immediate Access" option from the left sidebar.
3. Click the Immediate Access link on the right-hand side.
4. The Bedford Bookshelf page will appear.
5. Click "Read Now".
6. Sign in to your Bookshelf account.
   a. If you do not have a Bookshelf account, create one using your CBU credentials.
   b. Use your NAME@calbaptist.edu email address.
   c. Do NOT use your ID@calbaptist.edu email address.
7. Enter the title of the material your course will be using in the search field at the top (Ex. Hello, Writer).
   a. Click → Open Book on the right-hand side to begin reading your material.

LOGIN REQUIREMENTS:
- Use your NAME@calbaptist.edu email address.
- Do NOT use your ID@calbaptist.edu email address.
- Using an ID email may prevent receipt of external emails such as VitalSource verification messages.

EXPECTED RESULT:
The Bedford Bookshelf library will load and allow access to the assigned course materials.

IF ISSUE PERSISTS:
Contact ImmediateAccess@calbaptist.edu for assistance. Please send your email from your LancerMail address and include your name, ID#, and course info.
