PROBLEM:
Student cannot access their WileyPlus courseware, e-textbook, or assignments.

APPLIES TO:
Courses using WileyPlus digital courseware (commonly used in Math courses such as MAT 342).

BLACKBOARD LOCATION:
Tools tab (left side pane)

STEP-BY-STEP RESOLUTION:
1. Log onto Canvas.
2. Select your course that uses WileyPlus.
3. Click "Tools" from the left side menu.
4. Find and click on "Wiley Course Resources" from the Tools list.
5. You will be presented with two options:
   - Option 1: Click "Login to an Existing Account" if you already have a WileyPlus account.
   - Option 2: Create a new account by filling in the registration form.
6. If creating a new account, enter:
   - First Name
   - Last Name
   - Email Address (use your CBU email)
   - Confirm Email Address
   - Department (select your department)
7. Check the consent boxes for Terms of Use and Privacy Policy.
8. Click "Submit" to create your account.
9. A 4-digit verification code will be emailed to you.
10. Enter the 4-digit code when prompted to verify your account.

NAVIGATION INSIDE WILEYPLUS:
After logging in, you will see the Course Resources page with:
- Key Resources: Click "Launch" on the WileyPLUS eTextbook tile to access your textbook.
- Course-Wide Resources: General course materials.
- Chapter folders: Access content organized by chapter (e.g., Ch 1: First-Order ODEs, Ch 2: Second-Order Linear ODEs, etc.).

EXPECTED RESULT:
After entering the verification code, you will have access to your WileyPlus e-textbook and course resources organized by chapter.

IF ISSUE PERSISTS:
Contact ImmediateAccess@calbaptist.edu for assistance. Please send your email from your LancerMail address and include your name, ID#, and course info.
