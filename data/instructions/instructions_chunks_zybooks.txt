PROBLEM:
Student is opted into Immediate Access but cannot access their Zybooks textbook or needs help finding their access code.

APPLIES TO:
Courses using Zybooks digital textbooks (commonly used in EGR courses such as Intro to Computer Programming in Python).

BLACKBOARD LOCATION:
Immediate Access tab (left side pane)

STEP-BY-STEP RESOLUTION:
1. Log onto Canvas.
2. Select the course in which you are enrolled that uses Zybooks.
3. Click "Immediate Access" from the left side pane.
4. Click "Immediate Access" once more from the center of the page.
5. Click the "Reveal Access Code" button.
6. Click "Copy" to copy the access code displayed in the field.
7. Navigate to the Zybooks URL provided on the page (example: https://learn.zybooks.com/zybook/CALBAPTISTEGR120CorsoSpring2026).
8. Paste your unique access code on the Zybooks website to activate your content.

EXPECTED RESULT:
After entering the access code on the Zybooks website, you will have full access to the Zybooks content for your course.

IF ISSUE PERSISTS:
Contact ImmediateAccess@calbaptist.edu for assistance. Please send your email from your LancerMail address and include your name, ID#, and course info.
