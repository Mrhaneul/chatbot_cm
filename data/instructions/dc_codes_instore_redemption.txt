PROBLEM:
Student purchased a digital content (DC) code in-store at the Campus Store and needs to redeem it to access their eBook or courseware.

APPLIES TO:
Digital content codes purchased in-person at the CBU Campus Store (not through Immediate Access). This includes access codes for various publishers that were bought as physical cards or printed on receipts.

ACCESS LOCATION:
Campus Store Website - Bookstore.Calbaptist.edu

STEP-BY-STEP RESOLUTION:
1. Go to the Campus Store website at Bookstore.Calbaptist.edu
2. Click the Profile Icon in the top right-hand corner of the page.
3. Log into an existing account OR create a new account:
   - Use your CBU email: firstname.lastname@calbaptist.edu
   - Do NOT use your ID#@calbaptist.edu email address.
4. Once logged in, click "Activate and Access eBooks" from your account menu.
5. Click "Click here to activate an in-store purchase."
6. Enter the Redemption Code found at the bottom of your receipt.
7. Click "Activate" to redeem the code.
8. Once redeemed, your book will appear under "Other Digital Titles."
9. Click the dropdown "Access Content" next to your book.
10. Click "Access" to open the content page.
11. On the book cover page, look for a small pop-up in the top right corner. Click "Instructions."
12. A pop-up menu will appear. Click "Reveal Access Code."
13. Copy the access code displayed.
14. Click "Go to Website" under the access code OR visit the link provided by your instructor.
15. Paste your access code on the publisher's website to activate your materials.

IMPORTANT NOTES:
- Some items cannot be returned once the code is revealed. Review the Return Policy before revealing your code.
- A Course Key may also be required for some materials. This may be found on your receipt or obtained from your instructor.
- The code must be redeemed on the publisher's or third-party website.

EXPECTED RESULT:
After redeeming your code on the publisher's website, you should have full access to your digital textbook or courseware.

IF ISSUE PERSISTS:
Contact the Campus Store Support Team at CSContact@calbaptist.edu for assistance.
