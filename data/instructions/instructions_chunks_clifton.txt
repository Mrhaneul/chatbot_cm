PROBLEM:
Student is opted into Immediate Access but cannot access their CliftonStrengths Assessment or needs help redeeming their access code.

APPLIES TO:
Courses using the CliftonStrengths for Students assessment by Gallup.

BLACKBOARD LOCATION:
Immediate Access tab

STEP-BY-STEP RESOLUTION:
1. Navigate to your course's Immediate Access page in Canvas.
2. Click "Reveal Access Code" to display your unique CliftonStrengths access code.
3. Copy your access code.
4. Click "Read Now" on the textbook tile to open the CliftonStrengths for Students e-book.
5. In the e-book, click the tab titled "IMPORTANT Information About Taking the CliftonStrengths Assessment."
6. Locate the link to redeem your code: press.gallup.com/students
7. On the Gallup website, under the "For Students" section, click "Redeem Code."
8. Paste your access code into the Access Code field.
9. Click "Continue" to proceed.
10. If prompted, create a Gallup account to complete the redemption process.

IMPORTANT NOTES:
- Your access code is unique and one-time-use only.
- The access code is valid for one use only, so do not share it.
- Make sure to enter the code exactly as it appears.

EXPECTED RESULT:
After redeeming your access code, you will have access to take the CliftonStrengths Assessment and view your results.

IF ISSUE PERSISTS:
Contact ImmediateAccess@calbaptist.edu for assistance. Please send your email from your LancerMail address and include your name, ID#, and course info.
