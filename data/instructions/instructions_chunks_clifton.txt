PROBLEM:
Student cannot access CliftonStrengths through Canvas Immediate Access.

APPLIES TO:
Platform: CliftonStrengths
LMS: Canvas

CANVAS LOCATION:
Course Navigation > Immediate Access, then use the textbook tile to reveal the access code and open the CliftonStrengths material.

STEP-BY-STEP RESOLUTION:
1. Log onto Canvas and click into the course using Clifton StrengthsFinder through IA.
2. On the left-hand side menu, click → Immediate Access
3. Copy your Clifton StrengthsFinder Access Code by clicking → Reveal Access Code.
4. Once you’ve copied your unique access code, click → Read Now on the textbook tile.
5. Click the tab titled IMPORTANT Information About Taking the Clifton StrengthsFinder.
   a. Locate the appropriate link to input your Access code (i.e. strengths.gallup.com)
6. Under the For Students option, click → Redeem Code.
7. Paste your access code into the given field, then click → Continue.

EXPECTED RESULT:
Student successfully accesses CliftonStrengths content through Canvas.

IF ISSUE PERSISTS:
Contact ImmediateAccess@calbaptist.edu for assistance. If possible, include a screenshot of what you are seeing.

[META:{"source_file": "ia_cliftonstrengths_assessment_access.txt", "source_id": "ia_cliftonstrengths_assessment_access", "source_type": "instruction", "platform": ["clifton"], "section_title": "FULL_DOCUMENT"}]
PROBLEM:
Student is opted into Immediate Access but cannot access their CliftonStrengths Assessment or needs help redeeming their access code.

APPLIES TO:
Courses using the CliftonStrengths for Students assessment by Gallup.

CANVAS LOCATION:
Immediate Access tab

STEP-BY-STEP RESOLUTION:
1. Navigate to your course's Immediate Access page in Canvas.
2. Click "Reveal Access Code" to display your unique CliftonStrengths access code.
3. Copy your access code.
4. Click "Read Now" on the textbook tile to open the CliftonStrengths for Students e-book.
5. In the e-book, click the tab titled "IMPORTANT Information About Taking the CliftonStrengths Assessment."
6. Locate the link to redeem your code: press.gallup.com/students
7. On the Gallup website, under the "For Students" section, click "Redeem Code."
8. Paste your access code into the Access Code field.
9. Click "Continue" to proceed.
10. If prompted, create a Gallup account to complete the redemption process.

IMPORTANT NOTES:
- Your access code is unique and one-time-use only.
- The access code is valid for one use only, so do not share it.
- Make sure to enter the code exactly as it appears.

EXPECTED RESULT:
After redeeming your access code, you will have access to take the CliftonStrengths Assessment and view your results.

IF ISSUE PERSISTS:
Contact ImmediateAccess@calbaptist.edu for assistance. If possible, include a screenshot of what you are seeing.
