PROBLEM:
Student is opted into Immediate Access but cannot access their Pearson MyLab or Modified Mastering textbook and assignments.

APPLIES TO:
Courses using Pearson MyLab or Modified Mastering digital content.

BLACKBOARD LOCATION:
Immediate Access tab (left side pane)

STEP-BY-STEP RESOLUTION:
1. Log onto Canvas.
2. Select the course that uses Pearson MyLab or Mastering.
3. Click "Immediate Access" from the left side menu.
4. Click "Immediate Access" again from the center of the page.
5. Click the "Launch Courseware" button.
6. Click "Continue" on the Legal Policies page to accept the terms.
7. Click "Open MyLab & Mastering" to access the Pearson platform.
8. If you are new to Pearson, click "Create account" to register.
9. Fill in your account information:
   - First name and Last name
   - Email (use your CBU email)
   - Username
   - Password
   - Country
   - Role (select Student)
10. Agree to the Terms of Use and Privacy notice.
11. Click "Create account" to complete registration.

LOGIN REQUIREMENTS:
- Use your official CBU email address: firstname.lastname@calbaptist.edu
- Do NOT use your ID#@calbaptist.edu email.

EXPECTED RESULT:
After registration, you will see a confirmation page showing:
1. "You're done!" message
2. Your course information and Professor's name
3. A "Go to My Courses" button to access your materials

You will also receive a registration confirmation email. Your Pearson account will be linked to your Canvas account for future access.

IF ISSUE PERSISTS:
Use the "Forgot your Pearson username or password?" link on the login page, or visit the Help & Support section in Pearson. You can also contact ImmediateAccess@calbaptist.edu for assistance. Please send your email from your LancerMail address and include your name, ID#, and course info.
