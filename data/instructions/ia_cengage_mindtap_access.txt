PROBLEM:
Student is opted into Immediate Access but cannot access Cengage MindTap textbook or assignments.

APPLIES TO:
Courses using Cengage MindTap.

CANVAS LOCATION:
Course Navigation > Immediate Access

STEP-BY-STEP RESOLUTION:
1. Sign onto Canvas and go to the course using Cengage MindTap/CNowV2.
2. Click the → Immediate Access link on the left-hand side course menu.
3. On the right-hand side, the Cengage MindTap/CNowV2 material should appear with an option to ‘Launch Courseware’. Click → Launch Courseware.
4. You will be pathed to the Cengage site, where you will be prompted to log into a Cengage account.
   a. If you do not have an existing account, create one with your Name@calbaptist.edu email address.
   b. DO NOT USE YOUR ID#@calbaptist.edu EMAIL.
   c. If you have an existing account, log in with those credentials.
5. Once logged in, you should be directly pathed to your MindTap/CNowV2 course, where you will be able to access your eText and courseware assignments.

LOGIN REQUIREMENTS:
- Use your full CBU email address (firstname.lastname@calbaptist.edu).
- Do NOT use your ID#@calbaptist.edu email.

EXPECTED RESULT:
After logging in, the MindTap eText and assignments should be accessible.

IF ISSUE PERSISTS:
Contact ImmediateAccess@calbaptist.edu for assistance. If possible, include a screenshot of what you are seeing.
