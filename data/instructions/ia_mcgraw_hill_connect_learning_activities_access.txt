PROBLEM:
Student cannot access their McGraw Hill Connect eTextbook or courseware through the Immediate Access tab or Learning Activities in Canvas.

APPLIES TO:
Courses using McGraw Hill Connect digital content accessed via Immediate Access tab or Learning Activities (commonly used in Business courses such as BUS 463 Business and Organizational Management).

BLACKBOARD LOCATION:
Immediate Access tab OR Learning Activities tab (left side pane)

METHOD 1 - VIA IMMEDIATE ACCESS TAB:
1. Log into Canvas and go to your course page.
2. Click "Immediate Access" on the left-hand side menu.
3. Click the "Immediate Access" link in the center of the page.
4. Click the "McGraw Hill Connect LTIA Basic Link."
5. You will be taken to the McGraw Hill Connect course page with access to the eTextbook and assignments.

METHOD 2 - VIA LEARNING ACTIVITIES TAB:
1. Log into Canvas and go to your course page.
2. Click "Learning Activities" on the left-hand side menu.
3. IMPORTANT: Complete the Student Responsibility Agreement (SRA) first before accessing other content.
4. Click on the weekly folder to view that week's assignments (e.g., "Week 1: Overview of Organizational Behavior").
5. Find the "McGraw Hill Connect" folder within the week.
6. Click on individual assignment links (SmartBook, Quiz, Connect Orientation Videos, etc.).

LOGIN REQUIREMENTS:
- If prompted to log in on the McGraw Hill website, use an existing McGraw Hill account OR create a new account.
- Use your CBU email: firstname.lastname@calbaptist.edu
- Do NOT use your CBUID#@calbaptist.edu email address.

ACCESSING THE EBOOK WITHIN AN ASSIGNMENT:
1. In the assignment, click "Continue" on the bottom right-hand side.
2. Click "eBook" on the left-hand side of the page.
3. A pop-up will appear on the right-hand side showing the "Browse eBook" option.
4. Click on the eBook cover to open and read.

NAVIGATION INSIDE MCGRAW HILL CONNECT:
- Course materials section shows the textbook and loose leaf options.
- Read section displays your eTextbook.
- Assignments are organized by weekly folders.
- You can download the mobile app to read offline.

EXPECTED RESULT:
After following these steps, you should have access to both the McGraw Hill Connect eTextbook and weekly assignments.

IF ISSUE PERSISTS:
Contact ImmediateAccess@calbaptist.edu for assistance. Please send your email from your LancerMail address and include your name, ID#, and course info.
