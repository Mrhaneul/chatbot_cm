PROBLEM:
Student cannot access CliftonStrengths through Canvas Immediate Access.

APPLIES TO:
Platform: CliftonStrengths
LMS: Canvas

CANVAS LOCATION:
Course Navigation > Immediate Access, then use the textbook tile to reveal the access code and open the CliftonStrengths material.

STEP-BY-STEP RESOLUTION:
1. Log onto Canvas and click into the course using Clifton StrengthsFinder through IA.
2. On the left-hand side menu, click → Immediate Access
3. Copy your Clifton StrengthsFinder Access Code by clicking → Reveal Access Code.
4. Once you’ve copied your unique access code, click → Read Now on the textbook tile.
5. Click the tab titled IMPORTANT Information About Taking the Clifton StrengthsFinder.
   a. Locate the appropriate link to input your Access code (i.e. strengths.gallup.com)
6. Under the For Students option, click → Redeem Code.
7. Paste your access code into the given field, then click → Continue.

EXPECTED RESULT:
Student successfully accesses CliftonStrengths content through Canvas.
