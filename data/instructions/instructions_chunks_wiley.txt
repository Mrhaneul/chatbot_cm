PROBLEM:
Student cannot access their WileyPlus courseware, e-textbook, or assignments.

APPLIES TO:
Courses using WileyPlus digital courseware (commonly used in Math courses such as MAT 342).

CANVAS LOCATION:
Course Navigation > WileyPLUS

STEP-BY-STEP RESOLUTION:
1. Log onto Canvas and select the course using WileyPLUS courseware.
2. Find and click on the WileyPLUS tool on the left-hand side.
3. Students will be presented with the choice to either:
   1) Login to an existing account
   OR
   2) Create a new account
4. If a new account was created, then a 4-digit code will be sent to the registered email address.
5. Copy and enter the emailed code into the WileyPLUS page.
   - Once the code is successfully redeemed, the material should become available.

NAVIGATION INSIDE WILEYPLUS:
After logging in, you will see the Course Resources page with:
- Key Resources: Click "Launch" on the WileyPLUS eTextbook tile to access your textbook.
- Course-Wide Resources: General course materials.
- Chapter folders: Access content organized by chapter (e.g., Ch 1: First-Order ODEs, Ch 2: Second-Order Linear ODEs, etc.).

EXPECTED RESULT:
After entering the verification code, you will have access to your WileyPlus e-textbook and course resources organized by chapter.

IF ISSUE PERSISTS:
Contact ImmediateAccess@calbaptist.edu for assistance. If possible, include a screenshot of what you are seeing.
