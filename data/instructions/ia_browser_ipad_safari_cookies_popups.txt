PROBLEM:
Immediate Access content does not load on iPad due to Safari cookie or pop-up restrictions.

APPLIES TO:
Students using Safari on iPad.

STEP-BY-STEP RESOLUTION:
1. Open the Settings app.
2. Scroll down and tap "Apps".
3. Select "Safari".
4. In the General section, toggle Block Pop-ups to OFF.
5. Scroll to Privacy & Security.
6. Toggle Prevent Cross-Site Tracking to OFF.
7. Scroll down and tap "Advanced".
8. Toggle Block All Cookies to OFF.

EXPECTED RESULT:
Immediate Access content should load properly on iPad.

IF ISSUE PERSISTS:
Contact ImmediateAccess@calbaptist.edu for assistance. Please send your email from your LancerMail address and include your name, ID#, and course info.
