PROBLEM:
Immediate Access content does not load on iPad due to Safari cookie or pop-up restrictions.

APPLIES TO:
Students using Safari on iPad.

STEP-BY-STEP RESOLUTION:
1. Open → Settings app.
2. Scroll down to the bottom. Click → Apps tab.
3. On the right-hand side, find and select → Safari app.
4. Scroll to the General section and toggle Block Pop-ups to OFF.
5. Scroll to the Privacy & Security section and toggle Prevent Cross-Site Tracking to OFF.
6. Scroll down and click into the Advanced section.
7. In the Privacy section, toggle Block All Cookies to OFF.

EXPECTED RESULT:
Immediate Access content should load properly on iPad.

IF ISSUE PERSISTS:
Contact ImmediateAccess@calbaptist.edu for assistance. If possible, include a screenshot of what you are seeing.
