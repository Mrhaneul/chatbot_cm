PROBLEM:
Student is opted into Immediate Access but cannot access their SimuCase simulation platform or needs help redeeming their access code.

APPLIES TO:
Courses using the SimuCase simulation platform (commonly used in Social Work courses such as SWK 335 Substance Use Disorders, Treatment, Recovery).

BLACKBOARD LOCATION:
Immediate Access tab (left side pane)

STEP-BY-STEP RESOLUTION:
1. Log into Canvas.
2. Select the course that uses SimuCase.
3. Click "Immediate Access" from the left-hand side pane.
4. Click "Immediate Access" again from the center of the page.
5. Click "Reveal Access Code" to display your unique SimuCase code.
6. Copy the access code that appears.
7. Copy the publisher's URL appearing under "More Info" (https://www.simucase.com/members/register/simucase).
8. Open a new browser tab and paste the URL to navigate to the SimuCase registration page.
9. On the SimuCase website, click "Accept All" on the Privacy Choices popup.
10. Fill in your account information:
    - First Name and Last Name
    - Primary Email Address (use your CBU email)
    - Secondary Email Address (optional personal email)
    - Create Password
    - Area of Focus (e.g., Social Work)
    - Select "Yes" for educational institution affiliation
    - University: California Baptist University
    - Role: Undergraduate Student
    - Graduation Date
11. Click "Continue" to proceed to the Payment page.
12. On the Payment page, navigate back to your Canvas page.
13. Click "Copy" to copy the unique access code from Canvas.
14. Return to the SimuCase page and paste your code in the "Promo Code" field.
15. Click "Apply" to redeem your access code.

IMPORTANT NOTES:
- Your access code functions as a Promo Code on the SimuCase payment page.
- The code covers your membership cost through Immediate Access.
- Do not select a paid membership option - use your Promo Code instead.
