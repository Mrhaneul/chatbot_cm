PROBLEM:
Student cannot access Jones & Bartlett Navigate through Canvas Immediate Access.

APPLIES TO:
Platform: Jones & Bartlett Navigate
LMS: Canvas

CANVAS LOCATION:
Course Navigation > Immediate Access, then use the textbook tile to reveal the access code and open the provided URL.

STEP-BY-STEP RESOLUTION:
1. Sign onto Canvas and go to the course using IA.
2. Click the → Immediate Access link.
3. Under the textbook tile, click → Reveal Access Code.
4. Copy & Paste the provided URL into a new tab.
5. Copy the access code provided to your clipboard.
6. In the new Jones & Bartlett tab, read and accept the terms and click → Accept.
7. Enter the code from Canvas into the required field and click → Redeem.
8. Log in or create a new account with Jones & Bartlett Learning using your CBU credentials.
   - Use your Name@calbaptist.edu email address
   - DO NOT use your ID#@calbaptist.edu email, as you will not receive necessary verification emails that may be sent to the email.
9. Once the account is created and the access code is successfully redeemed, you should be able to access your Jones & Bartlett Navigate material.

EXPECTED RESULT:
Student successfully accesses Jones & Bartlett Navigate content through Canvas.

IF ISSUE PERSISTS:
Contact ImmediateAccess@calbaptist.edu for assistance. If possible, include a screenshot of what you are seeing.
