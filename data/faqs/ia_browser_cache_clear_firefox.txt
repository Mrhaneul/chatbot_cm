QUESTION:
I am trying to access my Immediate Access materials through Canvas but I see "0 Courses, 0 Materials" or get an error that says "You currently have no content available." I am using Mozilla Firefox on a Mac. How do I fix this?

ANSWER:
This is usually caused by stale browser cookies or cached data interfering with your Immediate Access session. You will need to clear your browser's cookies, cache, and history in Firefox, then try again.

Note: After clearing your browsing data, you will be signed out of most websites and will need to sign back in.

STEP-BY-STEP RESOLUTION:

1. From the top menu bar, click History and select Clear Recent History.
2. Under Time range to clear, select Everything.
3. Check the box for Cache (and any other items you wish to clear).
4. Click OK.

Shortcut: Press Command + Shift + Delete (Mac) to open the Clear All History menu directly.

EXPECTED RESULT:
After clearing your browsing data, return to Canvas, navigate to your course, and click the Immediate Access link again. Your materials should now load correctly.

If you continue to experience issues, please contact the CBU Campus Store at ImmediateAccess@calbaptist.edu for further assistance.
