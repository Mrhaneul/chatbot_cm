QUESTION:
What is the CBU Campus Store in store pickup policy?

ANSWER:
If you choose In Store Pickup as your shipping method, the Campus Store will notify you by email when your order is ready.

PICKUP WINDOW:
Packages are held at the store free of charge for 14 days once your order has been processed.

UNCLAIMED PACKAGES (after 14 days):
- General Merchandise, Textbooks, and Tradebooks: refunded to the payment method on file with a 25% restocking fee deducted
- Apple and Technology products: refunded to the payment method on file with a 10% restocking fee deducted

The following items will NOT be refunded if left unclaimed:
- Orders purchased using a book voucher
- Access codes and other digital content
- Pre-paid items (specially ordered and considered final sale)
- Rented textbooks: if left in unclaimed packages by the end of the semester rental return deadline, the textbooks will be returned at the register to avoid late fees but will not be refunded — no exceptions

PICKUP EXTENSION:
You may request an extension of the pickup window to 30 days from the original order processing date by filling out the In Store Pickup Extension Form before the original 14-day deadline.

Important conditions for extensions:
- You must fill out the In Store Pickup Extension Form before the original 14-day deadline
- If an extension is granted, the order will be considered FINAL SALE and will no longer be eligible for return or exchange
