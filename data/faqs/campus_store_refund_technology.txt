QUESTION:
What is the return and refund policy for technology and Apple products at the CBU Campus Store?

ANSWER:
All technology merchandise must be in resalable condition in original unopened packaging with manuals and warranties, accompanied by the original receipt.

RETURN WINDOW:
- All returns must be made within 5 days of purchase or pickup
- Original receipt and unopened packaging are required unless the item is defective
- Defective merchandise must be returned within 10 business days of purchase

RESTOCKING FEE:
A 10% restocking fee may be assessed on all returns. Refunds are issued only to the account used to place the original order.

CONDITIONS:
- No returns on closeout, clearance, or opened box stock unless the item is defective
- Defective merchandise is subject to testing before return approval is given
- Defective software must be exchanged with the original manufacturer
- Special-order items are non-returnable unless determined to be defective

TECHNOLOGY PRODUCTS COVERED:
Technology products include but are not limited to:
- Apple products (excluding charging cables)
- Computers and tablets
- Monitors, speakers, headphones and earbuds
- Printers and cameras
