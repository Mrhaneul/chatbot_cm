QUESTION:
What is the CBU Campus Store shipping policy?

ANSWER:
The CBU Campus Store offers competitive shipping rates. All shipments are professionally packed to ensure the safety of your items.

DELIVERY TIME:
On average, orders are received within 3 to 5 working days. During peak periods (August–September or December–January), delivery times may take longer.

FLAT RATE SHIPPING:
The CBU Campus Store offers $12.95 Flat Rate Standard Shipping on all textbook and merchandise orders.

Conditions for flat rate shipping:
- Available for U.S. orders only
- Not transferable and not redeemable for cash
- Available for Standard Shipping on valid U.S. addresses only
- Diploma frames, ART kits, and ARC kits are excluded from this promotion

ADDRESS REQUIREMENTS:
Please confirm you are entering a valid U.S. shipping address before placing your order. Orders that result in an insufficient address or require an address adjustment after shipment will incur an additional shipping charge.

Note: All shipping prices are subject to change. Orders over $500 may require a signature upon delivery unless stated otherwise.
