Textbook Refund Policy – FAQ
2025–2026 Academic School Year

[FAQ_1]
1. Can I return my textbooks by shipping them back?
Yes. You may return textbook purchases using a shipping method of your choice at your own expense. It is strongly recommended that you insure your package when returning items by mail.
Article link: "https://example.com/textbook-returns"

[FAQ_2]
2. Can I return textbooks in person at the CBU Campus Store?
Yes. Textbook purchases may be returned in person at the CBU Campus Store, subject to the return conditions and deadlines outlined below.
Article link: "https://example.com/textbook-returns"

[FAQ_3]
3. What is the return policy for Fall 2025 semester textbooks?
For textbooks purchased for Fall 2025 semester courses:
- Returns and exchanges are accepted without penalty until September 12, 2025.
- Returns are accepted with a 25% restocking fee from September 13, 2025 through September 19, 2025.
- All textbook sales are final after September 19, 2025.
Article link: "https://example.com/textbook-returns"

[FAQ_4]
4. What is the return policy for Spring 2026 semester textbooks?
For textbooks purchased for Spring 2026 semester courses:
- Returns and exchanges are accepted without penalty until January 23, 2026.
- Returns are accepted with a 25% restocking fee from January 24, 2026 through January 30, 2026.
- All textbook sales are final after January 30, 2026.
Article link: "https://example.com/textbook-returns"

[FAQ_5]
5. Which items are considered final sale and cannot be returned?
The following items are final sale and are not eligible for return or refund:
- ARC / ART / DES / ILL Kits
- Opened shrink-wrapped textbooks
- Access codes and digital content
- Lab coats
- Pre-paid items
- Items marked “Final Sale”
Article link: "https://example.com/textbook-returns"

[FAQ_6]
6. What condition must new textbooks be in to be eligible for return?
New textbooks must be in original condition, exactly as purchased, and must include all accompanying materials such as CDs, DVDs, or access codes.
Article link: "https://example.com/textbook-returns"

[FAQ_7]
7. What condition must used textbooks be in to be eligible for return?
Used textbooks must be in the same condition as when purchased and must include all accompanying materials such as CDs, DVDs, or access codes.
Article link: "https://example.com/textbook-returns"

[FAQ_8]
8. Are shrink-wrapped textbooks eligible for return if opened?
No. Shrink-wrapped textbooks cannot be returned if the shrink-wrapping has been opened or removed.
Article link: "https://example.com/textbook-returns"

[FAQ_9]
9. Are access codes or digital content refundable?
No. Access codes and digital content are final sale items and are not eligible for return or refund.
Article link: "https://example.com/textbook-returns"

[FAQ_10]
10. Where can I find more information about textbook returns?
For additional details, please refer to the Textbook FAQ page or contact the CBU Campus Store directly.
Article link: "https://example.com/textbook-returns"

[FAQ_11]
11. If I opt out of immediate access, are the physical textbooks for my courses available in the University’s student store?
No, the Campus Store does not have print alternatives to the textbooks delivered through Immediate Access. This prevents students who are looking for textbooks in-person from purchasing the same material they've already been charged for through Immediate Access.
If you'd like to procure print copies of your IA textbooks on your own, from your preferred textbook retailer, we've provided the Print ISBN of each title on our website.
Article link: "https://example.com/textbook-returns"
