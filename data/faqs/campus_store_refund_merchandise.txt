QUESTION:
What is the return and refund policy for general merchandise and trade books at the CBU Campus Store?

ANSWER:
All returns and exchanges must be in original condition and accompanied by the original receipt.

RETURN WINDOWS:
- Returns and exchanges accepted within 30 days from the date of purchase — no penalty
- Returns accepted with a 25% restocking fee between 31 and 60 days from the date of purchase
- All sales are FINAL after 60 days from the date of purchase

CONDITIONS FOR RETURN:
- Merchandise must be in original condition
- Altered, laundered, or unwrapped merchandise cannot be returned or exchanged
- Receipt and tags are required
- Items discounted at 50% or more are FINAL SALE
- Defective merchandise returned in original packaging will be exchanged for the identical item
- Pre-paid items are FINAL SALE
- Items marked "Final Sale" are non-refundable and non-exchangeable

HOW TO RETURN:
You may return items in person at the CBU Campus Store or by mail at your own expense. It is strongly recommended that you insure mailed returns.

Call ahead at 951-343-4259 or email cscontact@calbaptist.edu before returning an item.

Mail returns to:
California Baptist University
Attn: Campus Store
8432 Magnolia Avenue
Riverside, CA 92504

Please allow 1 to 3 weeks for the return to be received and processed.
