QUESTION:
I am trying to access my Immediate Access materials through Canvas but I see "0 Courses, 0 Materials" or get an error that says "You currently have no content available." I am using Google Chrome on an iPad. How do I fix this?

ANSWER:
This is usually caused by stale browser cookies or cached data interfering with your Immediate Access session. You will need to clear your browser's cookies, cache, and history in Chrome on your iPad, then try again.

Note: After clearing your browsing data, you will be signed out of most websites and will need to sign back in.

STEP-BY-STEP RESOLUTION:

1. Open Chrome on your iPad and tap the three dots (ellipsis) in the upper-right corner.
2. Swipe left on the top menu bar until you see History, then tap it.
3. At the bottom of the History screen, tap Clear Browsing Data.
4. Make sure Browsing History, Cookies, Site Data, and Cached Images and Files are all checked.
5. Tap Clear Browsing Data to confirm.

Note: If you see a VitalSource error message that says "Uh oh, your launch was unsuccessful!" and offers a Support Site button, skip that button — it will not resolve the issue. Proceed directly with clearing your browser data as described above.

EXPECTED RESULT:
After clearing your browsing data, return to Canvas, navigate to your course, and click the Immediate Access link again. Your materials should now load correctly.

If you continue to experience issues, please contact the CBU Campus Store at ImmediateAccess@calbaptist.edu for further assistance.
