QUESTION:
What are the terms for digital codes and licensing at the CBU Campus Store?

ANSWER:
When you purchase a digital product from the CBU Campus Store, you are licensing it for a set duration — not purchasing permanent ownership of the content.

Effective Date: January 1, 2025

LICENSE DURATION:
The duration of your license is clearly stated in the product description. Products labeled as "Lifetime" typically mean five (5) years of online access and permanent download to a supported device.

LICENSE CHARACTERISTICS:
- All licenses are non-transferable — they cannot be shared or assigned to another person
- The purchase grants you specific access rights as described in the product details
- Ownership of the underlying content remains with the publisher or creator

LIFETIME LICENSES INCLUDE:
- Online Access: access to the product on the platform for typically five (5) years
- Downloads: you may permanently download the product to a supported device
- Support and Maintenance: support for downloaded files is tied to compatibility with VitalSource supported devices and platforms at the time of download

DOWNLOADS:
You can download digital products for use on a supported device (the two most recent versions of an operating system). Downloads will function as long as VitalSource supports the device.

ACCESS CODES:
For products classified as Access Codes, including bundles with eBooks and Access Codes:
- These products are provided by a third party
- All information about these products including duration, licensing terms, and technical requirements can be found on the respective provider's website
- While the Campus Store facilitates your purchase, ongoing access and support are managed by the access code provider

IMPORTANT:
Access codes and digital content are FINAL SALE — they are not eligible for return or refund once purchased or redeemed.

QUESTION:
I am trying to access my Immediate Access materials through Canvas but I see "0 Courses, 0 Materials" or get an error that says "You currently have no content available." I am using Google Chrome on a Mac or PC. How do I fix this?

ANSWER:
This is usually caused by stale browser cookies or cached data interfering with your Immediate Access session. You will need to clear your browser's cookies, cache, and history in Google Chrome, then try again.

Note: After clearing your browsing data, you will be signed out of most websites and will need to sign back in.

STEP-BY-STEP RESOLUTION:

1. Click the three dots in the top-right corner of your Chrome window and select Settings.
2. On the left, choose Privacy and security, then select Clear browsing data.
3. Under Time range, select All time.
4. Check the boxes for Browsing history, Cookies and other site data, and Cached images and files.
5. Click Clear data.

Shortcut: Press Command + Shift + Delete (Mac) to open the Clear browsing data menu directly.

EXPECTED RESULT:
After clearing your browsing data, return to Canvas, navigate to your course, and click the Immediate Access link again. Your materials should now load correctly.

If you continue to experience issues, please contact the CBU Campus Store at ImmediateAccess@calbaptist.edu for further assistance.

QUESTION:
What should I do if a textbook is missing from my Immediate Access bundle?

ANSWER:
If a textbook you expected to see in your Immediate Access bundle is not showing up, there are a few possible reasons and steps to resolve it.

POSSIBLE REASONS:
- The course may use a publisher platform that delivers access through a direct link in Canvas rather than through the Immediate Access tab
- The textbook may not be included in the Immediate Access program for your specific course section
- Your enrollment in Immediate Access may not have processed yet

STEPS TO RESOLVE:

1. Check Canvas first:
   - Log into Canvas
   - Open the course that uses the missing textbook
   - Look for a publisher link in the course menu, under "Course Materials," "Learning Activities," or "Weekly Chapters"
   - Some platforms (McGraw Hill, Sage, Pearson) deliver access through these links, not through the IA tab

2. Confirm you are opted in:
   - Click the Immediate Access tab in Canvas
   - Verify your opt-in status for the course

3. Contact the Campus Store:
   If you have checked Canvas and confirmed your opt-in status but the textbook is still missing, contact:
   ImmediateAccess@calbaptist.edu

   If possible, include a screenshot of what you are seeing.

IF ISSUE PERSISTS:
Contact ImmediateAccess@calbaptist.edu for assistance. If possible, include a screenshot of what you are seeing.

QUESTION:
What is Immediate Access?

ANSWER:
Immediate Access is California Baptist University's program that provides day-one access to digital course materials through your student account.

**How It Works:**
- Get instant access to digital textbooks and courseware on the first day of class
- Materials are automatically available through Canvas
- Charges are billed directly to your student account
- You have 14 days to opt out if you prefer to purchase materials separately

**Key Benefits:**
- No waiting for materials to arrive
- No upfront payment needed
- Convenient access through Canvas
- Often more affordable than traditional textbooks

**Supported Platforms:**
Immediate Access works with major publishers including:
- Cengage (MindTap, Cengage Unlimited)
- McGraw Hill (Connect, ALEKS)
- Pearson (MyLab, Mastering)
- Bedford, Sage, VitalSource, and more

**Billing & Payment:**
- Charges appear on your student account statement
- Can be paid alongside tuition and fees
- Payment plans available through student accounts

**Opting Out:**
- 14-day opt-out period from the start of the semester
- Opt out through the Immediate Access page in Canvas
- After opting out, you must purchase materials separately

**Getting Help:**
If you're having trouble accessing your materials, please specify which platform you need help with (e.g., "I can't access Cengage MindTap" or "Help with McGraw Hill Connect").

QUESTION:
How do I opt out of Immediate Access?

ANSWER:
To opt out of Immediate Access in Canvas:

1. Log in to your CBU Canvas account using your student email and password.
2. Click Courses on the global navigation menu.
3. Open the homepage of the course using Immediate Access.
4. Click Immediate Access from the course menu on the left-hand side.
5. Locate the Immediate Access title or titles you wish to opt out of.
6. Click the "Want to Opt-Out" button.
7. Select the reason for opting out in the confirmation box.
8. Click Opt Out.
9. Verify that you have opted out of the materials. In the IA content block, confirm that the red caution symbol and "Opted Out" are visible.

Repeat these steps for every Immediate Access course you wish to opt out of.

If you are still having issues opting out, email optout@calbaptist.edu.

3. Check the box for Cache (and any other items you wish to clear).
