QUESTION:
What are the textbook purchasing terms of use at the CBU Campus Store?

ANSWER:
In compliance with the Higher Education Opportunity Act of 2008, California Baptist University and the CBU Campus Store provide up-to-date textbook and course material information for your classes.

IMPORTANT TERMS TO KNOW:

Textbook Information:
- Textbook and course material information on the website is provided by the academic departments and is updated daily as additions, changes, and cancellations are received
- Always check whether a textbook is listed as Optional, Pick One, or Required before purchasing

Pricing:
- The pricing of all textbooks is subject to change at any time and will be updated as stock arrives
- Any material listed at $0.00 has not yet been priced
- The Campus Store makes every effort to ensure pricing accuracy on the website

Third-Party Purchasing Risk:
- Because textbook information can change at any time, using this information to purchase textbooks from other sources is at your own risk
- California Baptist University and the CBU Campus Store cannot be held responsible for purchases made elsewhere based on this information
- Customers who purchase directly from the Campus Store are protected by and subject to the Campus Store return policy
