QUESTION:
How do I return something to the CBU Campus Store?

ANSWER:
The CBU Campus Store accepts returns in person or by mail, depending on the item. Before returning anything, please read the return policy for your specific item type to confirm it is eligible.

STEP 1 — CONTACT THE CAMPUS STORE FIRST:
Call 951-343-4259 or email cscontact@calbaptist.edu to let the team know you are returning an item and the reason for your return.

STEP 2 — PREPARE YOUR RETURN:
- Make sure your item is in the condition required by the return policy
- Include a copy of your original receipt or packing list
- Include all original materials that came with the item

STEP 3 — RETURN IN PERSON OR BY MAIL:

In Person:
Bring your item to the CBU Campus Store during store hours.
Address: 8432 Magnolia Avenue, Riverside, CA 92504

By Mail (at your expense):
Ship your return to:
California Baptist University
Attn: Campus Store
8432 Magnolia Avenue
Riverside, CA 92504

It is strongly recommended that you insure your package when returning by mail.

PROCESSING TIME:
- Allow 1 to 3 weeks for the return to be received and processed
- If you are expecting a replacement shipment, allow 2 to 4 weeks

RETURN POLICIES BY ITEM TYPE:
- Textbooks: see textbook refund policy for semester-specific deadlines
- General merchandise and trade books: returns accepted within 30 to 60 days depending on restocking fee
- Technology and Apple products: returns accepted within 5 days of purchase
- Access codes and digital content: FINAL SALE — not eligible for return or refund
- Pre-paid items: FINAL SALE
