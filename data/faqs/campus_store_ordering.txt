QUESTION:
How do I place an order with the CBU Campus Store?

ANSWER:
You can place orders securely through the CBU Campus Store website.

HOW ORDERING WORKS:

Shipping Orders:
Once your order is placed, the Campus Store will send you an email with tracking information after your items have been shipped.

In Store Pickup Orders:
If you choose In Store Pickup, you will receive an email when your order is ready for pickup.

Digital / Access Code Orders:
Some access codes will be delivered electronically via email regardless of the shipping method you selected. This is not immediate delivery and will still take the normal processing time.

PROCESSING TIME:
Please allow up to 48 hours for orders to be processed.

CONTACT:
If you have any questions about your order, contact the Campus Store at:
- Email: cscontact@calbaptist.edu
- Phone: 951-343-4259

Staff can also assist you in placing your order over the phone.
