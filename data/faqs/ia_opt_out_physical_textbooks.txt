QUESTION:
If I opt out of Immediate Access, can I buy a physical copy of my textbook from the Campus Store?

ANSWER:
No. The Campus Store does not carry print alternatives for textbooks that are delivered through Immediate Access. This policy prevents students from purchasing materials in-store that they may have already been charged for through Immediate Access.

Students who wish to obtain a physical copy of their Immediate Access textbook may purchase one independently from their preferred textbook retailer. The print ISBN for each Immediate Access title is available on the Campus Store website.
