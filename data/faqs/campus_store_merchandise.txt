QUESTION:
What does the Campus Store sell?

ANSWER:
The CBU Campus Store carries a wide variety of CBU-branded merchandise, apparel, technology, course supplies, and gifts. For the full selection, contact: cscontact@calbaptist.edu

ALUMNI:
- Alumni gear, accessories, and apparel

ATHLETICS:
- Nike headwear, polos, and shirts
- Athletic accessories, bottoms, headwear, outerwear, shirts, jerseys, tank tops

BOTTOMS:
- Lounge and pajamas, shorts, sweatpants, women's bottoms

COLLECTIONS:
- 75th Anniversary, best sellers, sale items
- CBU Family, CBU Grandparents, CBU Pets
- Disney x CBU, Hello Kitty x CBU, Peanuts x CBU
- Julia Gash Collection, March Madness, Christmas and seasonal

COURSE SUPPLIES:
- ARC and ART kits, general supplies, lab products
- Office and school supplies, planners, calendars, binders

DRINKWARE:
- Metal and plastic water bottles, mugs, tumblers

GIFTS AND ACCESSORIES:
- Backpacks, bags, decals, stickers, jewelry, pins
- Lanyards, keychains, plush, toys, socks, footwear
- Throw pillows, totes, wallets, insignia and commencement gifts

GRADUATION GEAR:
- Diploma frames, grad bundles, stoles

HEADWEAR:
- Beanies, hats, visors

OUTERWEAR:
- Crewnecks, full zips, hoodies, jackets, quarter zips, sweaters

SHIRTS:
- Long sleeves, polos, short sleeves, women's shirts

TECHNOLOGY:
- MacBook Air, MacBook Pro, iPads, Apple Pencils
- Beats headphones, chargers, adapters, Mac and iPad accessories

YOUTH:
- Infant and toddler items, youth accessories, bottoms, outerwear, shirts

For store hours and location, ask "What are the Campus Store hours?" or "Where is the Campus Store?"

For more information, contact: cscontact@calbaptist.edu
